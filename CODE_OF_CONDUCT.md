# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful and considerate
- Assume positive intent
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, or abuse
- Keep discussions professional and constructive

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this code.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers through their GitHub profiles.
